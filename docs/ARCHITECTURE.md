## Pages
1. Management & Profile Center
2. Centralized Modular Dashboard

## Layer 1
Provider adapters, management commands, training/data-ingestion orchestration and controlled Layer 2 service calls.

## Layer 2
Brain, memory, knowledge and persistence. No chat interface. Other layers use service contracts.

## Layer 3
Agent/bot runtime, operational chat, workflows and controlled integration/testing bridge.

## Safety
The main-project bridge is disabled by default. Initial bridge tests are read-only/contract tests. Production mutations require explicit adapters, permission policy and approval flow.
